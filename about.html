ABOUT HARISH SINGH

Design is more than how it looks.

I'm a Senior UI/UX and Product Designer with 11+ years of overall design experience, including 6+ years in UI/UX and Product Design and 5+ years in Graphic Design.

Over the course of my career, I've designed web apps, mobile products, SaaS platforms, eCommerce experiences, AI-driven applications, and design systems for client projects, startups, and product companies.

My background in graphic design gives me a distinct advantage: a strong foundation in visual communication, typography, composition, and brand identity that elevates every product experience I create.

✦

My Design Philosophy

"Great product design isn't just about beautiful interfaces — it's about solving real user problems, reducing friction, and driving measurable business growth."

I focus on user research, intuitive user flows, accessible design systems, and rapid prototyping to turn complex business ideas into seamless digital experiences.

WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY

My Design Process

How I take a digital product from initial concept to launch.

STEP 01

Discover

User research, stakeholder interviews, competitor benchmarking, and requirement mapping.

STEP 02

Define

Information architecture, user personas, journey mapping, and core task flow definition.

STEP 03

Design

Wireframing, design system tokenization, high-fidelity UI design, and interactive prototyping.

STEP 04

Validate

Usability testing, feedback iteration, accessibility audits, and design refinement.

STEP 05

Deliver

Developer handoff, design system documentation, component specs, and post-launch review.
